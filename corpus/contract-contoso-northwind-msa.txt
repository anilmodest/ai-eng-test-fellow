MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between Harbour Health Pharmaceuticals ("Harbour Health") and Juniper Schools Traders Ltd ("Juniper Schools"), effective
1 June 2026.

1. Scope. Juniper Schools provides data engineering consulting on a time-and-materials basis under
individual statements of work. Each statement of work states its own rate; the default rate is
175 USD per hour for a senior consultant and 120 USD per hour for a consultant.

2. Invoicing. Juniper Schools invoices monthly in arrears. Harbour Health pays within thirty (30) days.
Disputed lines must be raised in writing within ten (10) business days of the invoice date.

3. Expenses. Reasonable travel is reimbursable at cost when pre-approved in writing by the
Harbour Health engagement lead. Rail travel is expected for journeys within Great Britain.

4. Intellectual property. Deliverables created under a statement of work belong to Harbour Health on
payment. Juniper Schools retains its pre-existing tools and libraries.

5. Confidentiality. Both parties keep the other's confidential information for five (5) years
after the Agreement ends.

6. Term and termination. The Agreement runs until 31 May 2028 unless terminated earlier on
thirty (30) days notice. Statements of work in progress complete under their own terms.

7. Insurance. Juniper Schools holds professional indemnity insurance of not less than 2,000,000 GBP.

Signed for Harbour Health Pharmaceuticals: A. Mehta, Head of Data
Signed for Juniper Schools Traders Ltd: P. Sorensen, Director
